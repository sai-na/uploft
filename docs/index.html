Upload Tracker for Drive

Start a big upload to Google Drive, walk away, and find it finished when you're back.

Uploads that don't fall over

Resumes

Survives a closed tab or a crash

Close the tab, restart Chrome or let it crash. Reopen the uploader and it carries on from what Drive already received, not from zero.

Resumes

Rides out Wi-Fi drops

When the connection drops it waits for the network, retries with back-off, and continues from the last byte Drive confirmed.

Stays awake

No sleeping mid-upload

Keeps your computer awake and stops Chrome from unloading the tab while uploads run, then lets it sleep again when they finish.

Faster batches

Several files at once

Uploads up to six files side by side. Big files go up in pieces that grow on fast connections. Folders keep their structure.

No duplicates

Knows what's already there

Uploading the same folder again skips files it already put in Drive, or replaces them with the new version.

Drive tab

Tracks uploads you start in Drive

Shows a progress panel and notifications on drive.google.com, and can press Drive's Retry button for you when an upload fails.

How it works

  1. Install Upload Tracker for Drive from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click its toolbar icon, then Upload files to Drive.
  3. Sign in with Google and allow access to the files it uploads.
  4. Drop files or folders in. Uploads go into an Upload Tracker folder in My Drive, or any folder you choose.

What it asks for, and why

Permission Why
Google Drive: only files it uses (drive.file) To upload your files and create folders for them. It can only see files it created and folders you pick. It can't read the rest of your Drive.
Your data on drive.google.com To read Drive's own upload panel, so it can show progress, notify you and retry failed uploads.
Your data on www.googleapis.com To send files to the Google Drive API.
Notifications To tell you when uploads finish or fail.
Power To keep your computer awake while uploads run.
Identity To show Google's sign-in window.
Storage To remember your settings and unfinished uploads on your computer.
Active tab When you click the toolbar icon on a Drive folder, to offer that folder as the destination.

Privacy

Files go straight from your browser to Google. There is no server in between, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings and unfinished uploads are stored only in your browser.
