Upload Tracker for Drive

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Upload Tracker for Drive is a Chrome extension that uploads files to your Google Drive and tracks uploads you start on drive.google.com. It has no server of its own. Your files go straight from your browser to Google. There are no analytics, no ads and no third-party trackers, and nothing is sold or shared.

Access to your Google account

When you sign in to the uploader, the extension asks Google for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. Google describes it as "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app". With it, the extension can only see:

  • files and folders it created for you, and
  • folders you choose yourself in Google's folder picker.

It cannot read the rest of your Drive, your Gmail, your contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What is sent to Google

The extension talks only to Google, over HTTPS, and only to do what you ask:

  • Sign-in (accounts.google.com): to get an access token for the permission above.
  • Uploads (www.googleapis.com): the contents, names, types and last-modified times of the files you upload, and the names of the folders it creates to keep your folder structure.
  • Checking for files already uploaded: it lists the names, sizes and dates of files in the destination folder that it is allowed to see, so it doesn't upload the same file twice.
  • Your account details: it asks Google for your name, email address and profile picture, to show which account you are uploading to. Your picture is loaded from Google.
  • Sign-out (oauth2.googleapis.com): it asks Google to revoke the access token.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

The folder picker page

To upload into a folder other than the default one, the extension opens Google's folder picker on a page we host with GitHub Pages: https://sai-na.github.io/upload-tracker-for-drive/picker.html.

  • The extension gives a temporary access token only to that exact page.
  • The page passes the token to Google's picker, which it loads from apis.google.com, and sends the folder you choose (its ID and name) back to the extension.
  • The page stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere else.
  • Like any website host, GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address when the page loads; see GitHub's privacy statement.

What is stored on your computer

Everything below stays in your browser's storage for this extension. None of it is sent to us.

  • Access token, in chrome.storage.session. This is memory-only storage that Chrome clears when it closes. Tokens expire after about an hour.
  • Your account's name, email address and picture link, in chrome.storage.local, so the uploader can sign you back in silently.
  • The folder you chose to upload into (its Drive ID and name), in chrome.storage.local.
  • Your settings, in chrome.storage.sync. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome copies them to your other computers.
  • The upload queue, in IndexedDB, so uploads can continue after a closed tab or a restart. For each file it keeps:
    • the name, size, type and last-modified time;
    • the destination folder and its path within that folder;
    • how much has been uploaded;
    • Google's resumable-upload address for it, which Google keeps for about a week;
    • a file handle so you can resume without choosing the file again. Chrome asks for your permission before it is used.
    An entry is deleted once its file is uploaded, skipped or cancelled.
  • Uploads seen in the Drive tab, in chrome.storage.local: up to 100 recent entries (file name, size, progress and status) and where you placed the progress panel. The list is cleared the next time you open Drive if it is more than a day old.

The Drive tab tracker

On drive.google.com, the extension reads Drive's own upload panel in the page. It uses that to:

  • show progress;
  • notify you when uploads finish or fail;
  • click Drive's Retry button for you after a failure (you can turn this off in the toolbar popup);
  • while uploads run, keep your computer awake (also optional) and stop Chrome from unloading the tab.

This all happens inside your browser. The tracker sends nothing over the network, and notifications are shown by Chrome on your computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Upload Tracker for Drive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information from Google APIs is used only to upload your files to Google Drive and show you their progress. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone, and not read by any person.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the uploader: this deletes the access token, your account details and the chosen folder, and asks Google to revoke access. Unfinished uploads stay in the queue until you cancel them.
  • Uninstall the extension: Chrome deletes everything it stored.
  • You can also remove the extension's access at any time from your Google Account: Third-party apps & services.

Files you uploaded are yours and stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there.
