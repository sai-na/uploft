About Uploft

Uploft is a free Chrome extension, with public source code, for uploading to Google Drive without watching the tab. It is made by Sai Nath at Meru Apps.

Who makes it

Sai Nath is a product engineer at Meru Apps, an independent app studio in Kerala, India. Sai Nath designs and builds the extension and writes its upload guides. The source code is public on GitHub.

Uploft is not made by Google. Google Drive and Chrome are trademarks of Google LLC.

How the guides are written

Every Drive limit, menu path and rule in the guides comes from Google's own help or developer pages, linked where it is used and dated when it was checked. Everything the extension is said to do comes from its source code. Worked examples show the arithmetic, so you can check it yourself. Where Google Drive's own tools do the job, the posts say so.

Corrections

If a limit has changed or something is wrong, open an issue on GitHub. The page is fixed and its "Updated" date changes.
